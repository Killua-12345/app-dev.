🕷️ My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man

About the Movie

My favorite movie is Spider-Man. It is a superhero movie about a young man named Peter Parker who gets special powers after being bitten by a radioactive spider.

Main Character

- Peter Parker – The person behind the Spider-Man mask.
- Mary Jane Watson – Peter's love interest.
- Green Goblin – One of Spider-Man's enemies.

Why I Like Spider-Man

I like Spider-Man because he is brave, responsible, and always willing to help other people. Even though he faces many challenges, he never gives up.

> "With great power comes great responsibility."

 My Favorite Things About the Movie

1. The exciting action scenes.
2. Spider-Man's amazing superpowers.
3. The story teaches us to be responsible.
4. The fight scenes against villains.

Movie Rating

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5/5

Spider-Man is my favorite movie because it teaches us that being a hero means helping others and doing what is right.
